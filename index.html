Stage 1

Train the vocabulary

Byte-pair encoding starts from single characters and repeatedly merges the most frequent neighbouring pair. Every merge becomes a new token. The end of a word is marked with </w>.

Vocabulary (token and id)

Stage 2

Tokenize a sentence

The sentence is cut into words, then each word is rebuilt with the learned merges in the order they were learned. A word-level vocabulary has no answer for a word it has never seen. Subwords still do.
